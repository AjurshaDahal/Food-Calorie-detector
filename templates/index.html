How It Works

Our AI pipeline from image upload to calorie estimation

01
📸

Upload Image

Take a photo or upload an image of your food. Supports PNG, JPG, and WebP formats up to 16MB.

02
🧠

AI Detection

ResNet50 CNN, fine-tuned on Food-101, processes the image through deep learning to identify the food item with confidence scoring.

03
📊

Calorie Estimate

Detected food is mapped to our USDA-sourced nutritional database for instant calorie and macronutrient breakdown.
